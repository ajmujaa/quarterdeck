# Licensing

Quarterdeck is open source under the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE). This page says what that means now and what is planned, so nobody has to guess.

## What is open, and stays open

Everything in this repository: the dashboard, the crew, the Brain, Connections, Fleet, the command line (`qtdeck`), the packs, the sandbox and the security checks. You may use it, change it, run it at work and build on it, free of charge, including in commercial products, as the Apache License allows.

Versions already released under Apache-2.0 stay under Apache-2.0. A later change of plans does not take them back.

## What may be paid later

A team edition is planned (see issue #41, and #40 for compliance reports). It would add things that matter to groups and companies, not to one person on one machine, for example a shared dashboard, sign-in for several people, an audit trail, and compliance reports. Those would be a separate product, with its own terms, that works on top of this open core. Nothing is for sale today.

## Our intent

Our aim is to keep everything for one person on one machine open. Features for teams and organisations may be paid. We will try not to move a feature that is already here behind a payment, and if the plan changes we will say so on this page first.

## Contributing

By sending a change you agree that it is licensed under Apache-2.0, and you confirm you have the right to send it: add `Signed-off-by` to each commit (`git commit -s`). See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). Your code stays yours and stays under Apache-2.0.

If a future change needs a licence other than Apache-2.0 for the open core, contributors would be asked first. We are not planning one.
